## 一、决策与方向

> 场景标签：#决策
> 用户带着方向选择、人生决策、取舍纠结来时调用

### 有目的的人生
- **来源**：📖《The Book of Elon》| Part I: Living a Purposeful Life
- **类型**：原则
- **核心观点**：你可以选择不平凡，可以选择不遵从
- **原文**：You can choose to be not ordinary. You can choose not to conform to the conventions taught by your parents. It's possible for ordinary people to choose to be extraordinary.
- **场景标签**：#决策

### 做有用的事，别追求荣耀
- **来源**：📖《The Book of Elon》| Be Useful
- **类型**：原则
- **核心观点**：衡量成功的标准是「我做了多少有用的事」
- **原文**：Don't aspire to glory; aspire to work. The measure of success in my life is: "How many useful things can I get done?"
- **场景标签**：#决策

### 为让你兴奋的未来而战
- **来源**：📖《The Book of Elon》| Fight for the Future
- **类型**：原则
- **核心观点**：为让你对未来感到兴奋的事情而战
- **原文**：Fight for the things that make you excited about the future. The future will not get here fast enough unless we force it.
- **场景标签**：#决策

### 成功之前先痴迷
- **来源**：📖《The Book of Elon》| Obsess for Success
- **类型**：原则
- **核心观点**：做你喜欢的、对别人有用的事——这才是满足感
- **原文**：Doing something I enjoy, which is useful for other people—that gives me satisfaction. I didn't aspire to create companies as a kid. I just wanted to work on meaningful things.
- **场景标签**：#决策

### 在世界准备好之前就开始
- **来源**：📖《The Book of Elon》| Start Before the World Is Ready
- **类型**：原则
- **核心观点**：说出你相信的事，即使听起来疯狂
- **原文**：We say the things we believe, even when sometimes those things we believe are delusional. When you're building a radically new thing, most people will think you're crazy.
- **场景标签**：#决策 #表达

### 创造多于消耗
- **来源**：📖《The Book of Elon》| Create More than You Consume
- **类型**：原则
- **核心观点**：经济是正和游戏，创造比消耗多就是在增加财富
- **原文**：Examine your beliefs of the economy. The economy is a positive-sum game, a "grow the pie" situation. Those who assume the economy is zero-sum tend to make everyone worse off.
- **场景标签**：#决策 #产品

### 害怕也照样去做
- **来源**：📖《The Book of Elon》| Feel the Fear; Do It Anyway
- **类型**：原则
- **核心观点**：直视恐惧，恐惧就会消失
- **原文**：Look fear straight in the eye and it will disappear. The nature of fear is that people don't look at it. Look at it directly, and it won't seem as scary.
- **场景标签**：#决策 #心理

### 放弃斯坦福——两天退学
- **来源**：📖《The Book of Elon》| Becoming a Founder
- **类型**：真实故事
- **核心观点**：确定了方向就行动，别在安全选项里浪费时间
- **原文**：I was at Stanford for two days and thought, "I can come back to Stanford anytime. The internet is happening now." If you don't act on it, the moment could pass you by.
- **场景标签**：#决策

### 预期会失去一切
- **来源**：📖《The Book of Elon》| I Expected to Lose Everything
- **类型**：真实故事
- **核心观点**：重要的事就去做，即使胜算不到 10%
- **原文**：When something is important enough, you do it even if the odds are not in your favor. When I started them, I guessed both SpaceX and Tesla each had a probability of less than 10 percent to succeed.
- **场景标签**：#决策 #心理

### 别用投资回报率选人生方向
- **来源**：📖《The Book of Elon》| I Expected to Lose Everything
- **类型**：方法论
- **核心观点**：别按最佳商业机会排序，按重要问题排序
- **原文**：I don't look at ideas and ask, "What is the rank-ordered list of best business opportunities?" I look for problems that are important to fix for people now and for the future to be good.
- **场景标签**：#决策

### 乐观且可能错的，好过悲观且正确的
- **来源**：📖《The Book of Elon》| I Expected to Lose Everything
- **类型**：金句
- **核心观点**：乐观但错了，也好过悲观但对了
- **原文**：You'd rather be optimistic and wrong about the future than pessimistic and right. If you're pessimistic, you're going to be miserable. Might as well enjoy the journey.
- **场景标签**：#决策 #心理

### 趁没有负担的时候冒险
- **来源**：📖《The Book of Elon》| Companies to Start
- **类型**：方法论
- **核心观点**：有了家庭后冒险会影响全家，现在就去做
- **原文**：Take risks now. Especially before you have kids and other obligations. As you get older, your obligations increase. Take risks now, and do something bold. You won't regret it.
- **场景标签**：#决策

### 使命驱动 vs 利益驱动
- **来源**：📖《The Book of Elon》| The Mission to Protect the Planet
- **类型**：原则
- **核心观点**：只为赚钱做事的人，最难的时候会放弃
- **原文**：The purpose of Tesla was, and remains, accelerating the use of sustainable energy. That's not some silly hippie thing—it matters for everyone.
- **场景标签**：#决策

## 二、痛苦与韧性

> 场景标签：#心理
> 用户带着焦虑、自我怀疑、情绪低谷来时调用

### 拼命工作——这是代价
- **来源**：📖《The Book of Elon》| Work like Hell
- **类型**：原则
- **核心观点**：如果你想成为我，先想清楚你是否愿意付出这个代价
- **原文**：I am wired for war. If you think you want to be me or do the things I've done…I would not wish being me on anyone. If you could look inside my mind, you definitely would not want to be me. It looks fun from the outside, but it's not.
- **场景标签**：#心理

### 睡在工厂地板上
- **来源**：📖《The Book of Elon》| Sleep on the Factory Floor
- **类型**：真实故事
- **核心观点**：真正的百小时周是每天只睡六小时
- **原文**：I've done many many stretches of one-hundred-hour weeks—true one-hundred-hour weeks, sleeping roughly six hours per day. I slept on the floor of the factory so the team going through a hard time could see me on the floor and know I was not in some ivory tower.
- **场景标签**：#心理

### 逆境锻造力量
- **来源**：📖《The Book of Elon》| Adversity Forges Strength
- **类型**：原则
- **核心观点**：如果你正在经历地狱，继续走
- **原文**：There is a great quote from Winston Churchill: "If you're going through hell, keep going."
- **场景标签**：#心理

### 吃玻璃，凝视深渊
- **来源**：📖《The Book of Elon》| Eat Glass and Stare into the Abyss
- **类型**：真实故事
- **核心观点**：创业开头很有趣，然后是好几年的地狱
- **原文**：When starting a company, usually the beginning is fun. Then, it's hellish for a number of years. You have to be prepared to eat glass and stare into the abyss. If you don't chew glass and stare into the abyss, you can't build a great company.
- **场景标签**：#心理 #产品

### 2008 年濒死——把最后的钱分成两半
- **来源**：📖《The Book of Elon》| Keeping Tesla Alive
- **类型**：真实故事
- **核心观点**：两个孩子都快死了，你选择让哪个饿死？他选了两个都救
- **原文**：I faced one of the most difficult choices of my whole life. I had maybe $30-40 million left. I could put it all into one company, and the other would definitely die. Or split it—but then both might die. I couldn't bring myself to let either one die. So I split the money.
- **场景标签**：#心理 #决策

### 圣诞夜——最后一小时
- **来源**：📖《The Book of Elon》| Keeping Tesla Alive
- **类型**：真实故事
- **核心观点**：最后一小时拿到融资，否则两天后发不出工资
- **原文**：We closed that round at 6:00 p.m. on Christmas Eve 2008. It was the last hour of the last day possible. We would have bounced payroll two days after Christmas.
- **场景标签**：#心理

### 死亡离你只有几英寸
- **来源**：📖《The Book of Elon》| Keeping Tesla Alive
- **类型**：金句
- **核心观点**：死亡就在脸前面，唾沫都溅到你身上了
- **原文**：It's definitely stressful when death is inches from your face, trying to eat your face off—like right there and the foam is spattering on you.
- **场景标签**：#心理

### 理智的边缘——三年地狱
- **来源**：📖《The Book of Elon》| The Edge of Sanity
- **类型**：真实故事
- **核心观点**：工作到理智的边缘，没有别的办法
- **原文**：I worked to the edge of sanity. There wasn't any other way to make it work but three years of hell. From 2017 to 2019, I experienced the longest period of excruciating pain in my life. We were on the ragged edge of bankruptcy the entire time.
- **场景标签**：#心理

### 像印第安纳·琼斯一样跑
- **来源**：📖《The Book of Elon》| The Edge of Sanity
- **类型**：金句
- **核心观点**：巨石在后面追你，前面是悬崖，必须跳
- **原文**：I felt like Indiana Jones running through the temple. There's a huge boulder chasing you and you need to jump across a giant pit in the ground. If you slow down, the boulder will crush you, and if you don't make the leap, you'll die in the pit.
- **场景标签**：#心理

### 我永远不会放弃
- **来源**：📖《The Book of Elon》| Keeping SpaceX Alive
- **类型**：金句
- **核心观点**：除非我死了或者彻底失去行动能力
- **原文**：I don't ever give up. I'd have to be dead or completely incapacitated. There should be absolutely zero question that SpaceX will prevail. For my part, I will never give up and I mean never.
- **场景标签**：#心理

### 第四次发射——六周，最后一发火箭
- **来源**：📖《The Book of Elon》| Keeping SpaceX Alive
- **类型**：真实故事
- **核心观点**：三次失败后只够再射一次，给团队六周时间
- **原文**：I collected everyone in the conference room and said, "We have one last rocket. Get your shit together, go back to the island, and launch it. You have six weeks."
- **场景标签**：#心理 #产品

### 物理学不在乎你的感受
- **来源**：📖《The Book of Elon》| Feedback over Feelings
- **类型**：原则
- **核心观点**：想被人喜欢是一个真正的弱点
- **原文**：Physics does not care about hurt feelings. It cares about whether you got the rocket right. I think it's a real weakness to want to be liked. A real weakness. And I do not have that.
- **场景标签**：#心理 #表达

### 坏消息要大声说
- **来源**：📖《The Book of Elon》| Feedback over Feelings
- **类型**：原则
- **核心观点**：坏消息大声且频繁地说，好消息轻声说一次
- **原文**：All bad news should be given loudly and often. Good news can be said quietly and once. I give people hardcore feedback, but I try to always focus on the substance of the discussion.
- **场景标签**：#心理 #产品

### 输的次数够多，你就不会带感情了
- **来源**：📖《The Book of Elon》| Innovation Needs Permission to Fail
- **类型**：金句
- **核心观点**：习惯输了之后，每一局你都能冷静对待
- **原文**：You will lose. It will hurt the first fifty times. When you get used to losing, you will play each game with less emotion.
- **场景标签**：#心理

## 五、跨场景通用

> 以下卡片不属于单一场景，多个场景都会调用

### 第一性原理思维
- **来源**：📖《The Book of Elon》| First-Principles Thinking
- **类型**：方法论
- **核心观点**：从物理学出发推理，不类比别人怎么做
- **原文**：When you want to do something new, you have to apply the physics approach. Physics teaches you to reason from first principles rather than by analogy. You boil things down to the most fundamental truths and reason up from there.
- **场景标签**：#决策 #产品 #心理 #表达

### 痴迷于真相
- **来源**：📖《The Book of Elon》| Obsess over Truth
- **类型**：原则
- **核心观点**：起点是承认自己可能是错的
- **原文**：Start somewhere. Then be prepared to question your assumptions, fix what you did wrong, and adapt to reality. I am obsessed with truth. It matters more to get it right than to not look stupid.
- **场景标签**：#决策 #产品

### 追求「更少的错」而非「绝对正确」
- **来源**：📖《The Book of Elon》| Aspire to Be Less Wrong
- **类型**：方法论
- **核心观点**：目标不是正确，是比昨天少错一点
- **原文**：The mental tools of physics are powerful. They tell us to assume we're wrong and that our goal is to be less wrong. Aspire to be less wrong over time.
- **场景标签**：#决策 #产品

### 工程就是魔法
- **来源**：📖《The Book of Elon》| Engineering Is Magic
- **类型**：原则
- **核心观点**：工程是真正的魔法，谁不想当魔法师？
- **原文**：Engineering is, for all intents and purposes, magic, and who wouldn't want to be a magician?
- **场景标签**：#产品 #决策

### 紧迫感上瘾
- **来源**：📖《The Book of Elon》| Speed Is Both Offense and Defense
- **类型**：原则
- **核心观点**：唯一真正的货币是时间
- **原文**：The only true currency is time. The best offense and defense is speed.
- **场景标签**：#产品 #决策

### 承担责任
- **来源**：📖《The Book of Elon》| Take Responsibility
- **类型**：原则
- **核心观点**：我当 CEO 不是为了生活质量，是因为我对公司负责
- **原文**：I am CEO of these companies because I feel I'm responsible for them, not because it's the best thing for my quality of life. If something went wrong, I couldn't live with myself if I'd been uninvolved.
- **场景标签**：#决策 #心理

### 文明并非自动进步
- **来源**：📖《The Book of Elon》| Companies Drive Progress
- **类型**：原则
- **核心观点**：技术不会自动变好，需要聪明人拼命工作
- **原文**：Some people think technology automatically gets better every year. It does not. It only gets better if smart people work like crazy to make it better. Ancient Egypt built incredible pyramids and forgot how. Entropy is not on our side.
- **场景标签**：#决策 #产品
